# South Africa's Path Off the FATF Grey List: Institutional Coordination and Beneficial Ownership Reform

**Author**: Lehlohonolo Seokwe, MA Political Science  
**Published**: October 2026  
**Topic**: Financial Sector Regulation & Anti-Money Laundering (AML/CFT)  
**Read Time**: 6 min  

## Executive Summary
In February 2023, the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) placed South Africa on its "grey list" of jurisdictions under increased monitoring, citing deficits in anti-money laundering and combating the financing of terrorism (AML/CFT). This policy brief analyses the 22 action items designated by FATF, evaluating the legislative sprint undertaken by National Treasury, the Prudential Authority, the Financial Intelligence Centre (FIC), and the National Prosecuting Authority (NPA). We assess the remaining structural bottlenecks—specifically the operational effectiveness of beneficial ownership transparency registers and the sustained prosecution of complex financial state capture crimes—and outline the macroeconomic dividends of an anticipated exit in 2025/2026.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Legislative Compliance vs Operational Execution**: South Africa achieved technical compliance across nearly all 40 FATF recommendations within 18 months via the General Laws (Anti-Money Laundering and Combating Terrorism Financing) Amendment Act. However, FATF's evaluation prioritizes "Immediate Outcomes"—real-world investigation, asset recovery, and conviction statistics.
2. **The Beneficial Ownership Register Bottleneck**: The Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC) and the Master's Office established automated beneficial ownership registers. Ensuring comprehensive compliance among millions of private legal entities remains an ongoing administrative hurdle.
3. **Cross-Border Transaction Friction**: Grey-listing heightened correspondent banking due diligence, increasing compliance costs for South African commercial banks and adding estimated 20–50 bps friction costs to international capital transfers.
4. **Macroeconomic Upside of De-listing**: Re-establishing full FATF compliance will lower foreign funding costs, reduce sovereign risk premia, and facilitate inward foreign direct investment (FDI).

## Policy Recommendations for Regulators
- **Accelerate Inter-Agency Intelligence Sharing**: Expand joint task team powers between FIC, the Directorate for Priority Crime Investigation (Hawks), and SARS to track illicit financial flows.
- **Enforce CIPC Beneficial Ownership Compliance**: Impose automated administrative fines and deregistration notices for corporate entities failing to submit annual beneficial ownership declarations.
- **Resource Specialised Commercial Crime Courts**: Direct dedicated fiscus allocations to financial forensics training for public prosecutors handling cross-border illicit transfers.
