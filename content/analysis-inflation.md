# Consumer Price Inflation: The Transmission of Price Stability

**Indicator**: Headline Consumer Price Index (CPI) Inflation  
**Author**: Lehlohonolo Seokwe, MA Political Science  

### 1. Trend
South Africa experienced a significant disinflation cycle over 2024–2025. After peaking at 6.9% in 2022 amid global energy disruptions and supply chain bottlenecks, headline CPI fell below the SARB's preferred 4.5% midpoint, reaching the lower 3% territory. Core inflation (excluding food, fuel, and energy) has demonstrated broad-based moderation, validating the central bank's hawkish policy posture during the preceding tightening cycle.

### 2. Why It Matters
Inflation acts as a regressive tax, eroding the purchasing power of low-income households who allocate more than 35% of their total expenditure to essential food and transport. For institutional investors and financial policymakers, stable inflation anchors long-term nominal bond yields and provides the SARB's Monetary Policy Committee with policy room to ease real interest rates.

### 3. Who Is Affected
- **Low- and Middle-Income Consumers**: Immediate relief on essential food baskets (cereals, oils, poultry) and public transport fares.
- **Fixed-Income Pensioners**: Protection of real purchasing power against inflationary bracket creep.
- **Corporate Borrowers**: Reduced inventory holding costs and lower cost of working capital as benchmark rates soften.
